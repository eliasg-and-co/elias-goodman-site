doux

A music format that can't be played twice.

Finished stems, modulated live by whatever's actually happening around you. A blizzard over Brooklyn, the delayed L Train making you late for work, a match in progress. There's no canonical version. No master to license, nothing for a catalog fund to price as yield, nothing for a model to train on. What you just heard is gone.

Live environmental, financial, and sports data mapped onto a finished session in real time — through a Max for Live device that routes any stream to any parameter. Continuous data moves continuous parameters. Thresholds flip states. Events fire one-shots.
